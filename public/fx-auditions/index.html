Each clip has 16 beats of the effect, then 8 beats for the music and tails to settle. Playback levels are matched. Click another player to switch; only one plays at a time.

The editable playground is currently open in Ableton. Its final save is waiting for the Mac to be unlocked. The listening examples below are fully saved and ready.

Try them live in Ableton: open LiveMixer – FX Playground.als, select Main, and use Flicker, Dub, Dive, or Halo. Turn on Auto audition and press Play to cycle through a dry reference and the four effects every 16 beats. Dry / reset brings the music back and lets the tails fade.

These are starting points for your ears. Drum Flicker acts on drums; the other effects use textures and selected percussion. Bass and vocal stems bypass them. Octave shimmer adds no fixed musical key.
